For example, $S(\epsp,\PP_2)$ is replaced by the two terms
\[
(H,u,v,w)=(\epsp,4/5,5/4,1),\qquad(\SSS_2,3/5,5/3,1).
\]
Both satisfy $uv=w^2=1$, and the tree and forest budgets are
$1+1=2=T(S(\epsp,\PP_2))$ and $1+2=3=F(S(\epsp,\PP_2))$.
The replacement graphs have respectively one and two edges, whereas the
original has three. In coordinate order $(A,A+I,C,C+B)$, the four slacks are
\[
\left(\frac{2}{5}x^{2}-\frac{4}{5}x,\;\frac{2}{5}x^{2}-\frac{2}{5}x,\;x^{2}-\frac{23}{12}x,\;x^{2}-\frac{23}{12}x\right).
\]
After $x=2+t$, their coefficient sequences are
\[
\left((0,\frac{4}{5},\frac{2}{5}),\;(\frac{4}{5},\frac{6}{5},\frac{2}{5}),\;(\frac{1}{6},\frac{25}{12},1),\;(\frac{1}{6},\frac{25}{12},1)\right),
\]
all of which are nonnegative for $t\geq0$.
