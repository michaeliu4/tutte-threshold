The four exceptional representative cubics are listed below. For
$0\leq t\leq1$, a coefficient sequence $(a,b,c,d)$ with $d<0$ gives
\[
a+bt+ct^2+dt^3=a+bt+t^2\bigl((c+d)+(-d)(1-t)\bigr)\geq0.
\]
In every listed case $a,b,c+d\geq0$.
\begin{align*}
S(\PP_2,\PP_3),\ A:\quad &-\frac{1}{25}t^{3}+\frac{13}{100}t^{2}+\frac{21}{50}t\\
S(\PP_2,\PP_3),\ A+I:\quad &-\frac{1}{25}t^{3}+\frac{9}{100}t^{2}+\frac{67}{100}t+\frac{33}{50}\\
S(\PP_3,\PP_3),\ A:\quad &-\frac{1}{50}t^{3}+\frac{53}{100}t^{2}+\frac{119}{100}t+\frac{1}{10}\\
S(\PP_3,\PP_3),\ A+I:\quad &-\frac{1}{50}t^{3}+\frac{51}{100}t^{2}+\frac{89}{50}t+\frac{34}{25}\\
\end{align*}
